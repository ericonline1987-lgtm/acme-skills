# Building presentations

Follow `../../shared/style-guide.md` for voice and terminology.

## Process
1. **Define audience, goal, and length.** What should the audience believe or do at the end? How many minutes?
2. **Write the storyline first** as a list of slide headlines. Read them in order; they should tell the whole story.
3. **One idea per slide.** The headline is a full-sentence takeaway ("Churn fell 12% after onboarding changes"), not a topic label ("Churn").
4. **Support each headline** with one visual or at most three short bullets.
5. **Add speaker notes** with what to say, not what's already on the slide.
6. **Close with the ask** or the single thing to remember.

## Rule of thumb
About one slide per 1–2 minutes of talk time. Move detail to an appendix.
