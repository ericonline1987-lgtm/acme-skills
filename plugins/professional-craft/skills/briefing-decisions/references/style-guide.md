# Acme style guide

Shared by every skill in this repo. Edit here, not in individual skills.

## Voice
- Plain, direct, friendly. Write like you'd explain it to a smart colleague.
- Active voice. "We shipped the fix," not "The fix was shipped."
- Short sentences. Cut words that don't change the meaning.

## Formatting
- Headings in sentence case.
- Bullets for parallel items; prose for reasoning.
- Numbers: numerals for 10 and above, and for all metrics and money.
- Dates: "Oct 11, 2026" in prose; ISO (2026-10-11) in data and filenames.
