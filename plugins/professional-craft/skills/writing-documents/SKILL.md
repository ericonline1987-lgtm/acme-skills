# Writing documents

Follow `../../shared/style-guide.md` for voice, formatting, and terminology.

## Process
1. **Pin down the reader and the decision.** Who reads it, and what should they know, decide, or do afterward?
2. **Lead with the bottom line.** First paragraph states the conclusion or ask.
3. **Outline before drafting.** Headings that read as a summary on their own.
4. **Draft, then cut** about 20%: filler phrases, repeated points, hedges that don't carry real uncertainty.
5. **Check facts and numbers** against the source material. Flag anything unverified.

## Formats
- **Memo:** Bottom line → Context → Recommendation → Next steps.
- **Proposal:** Problem → Proposed approach → Cost / timeline → Risks → Ask.
- **Report:** Summary → Findings → Implications → Appendix.
