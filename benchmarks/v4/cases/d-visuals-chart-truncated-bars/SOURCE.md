## Grader basis

- rejects-truncated-bars: Datawrapper Academy and data.europa.eu (truncated bars break proportionality and exaggerate); FlowingData (bars encode by length).
- position-based-alternative: Datawrapper (dot plots/range plots may start above zero; plot differences); FT Visual Vocabulary (slope, dot plot for change between points).
- savannah-volume: standard analytic practice of checking the denominator before comparing rates (practitioner judgment, no single source).
- emphasis-on-message: Slideworks on action titles stating the so-what; FT Visual Vocabulary's framing of chart choice around the message.

## Why a capable general model might miss it

The requester has already chosen the truncated axis and asked "any objections", which invites agreement; and models rarely scan a supporting column (orders shipped) for a denominator problem unless asked.

The warehouse data is synthetic.
