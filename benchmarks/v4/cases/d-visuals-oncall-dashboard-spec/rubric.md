A senior SRE designs the dashboard around the question "are customers able to check out, and if not, where is it breaking?": a top row of SLO symptoms for POST /checkout (success ratio excluding 4xx, p50/p95/p99 from the histogram against 1.5s, traffic, error-budget remaining and multi-window burn rate), then a short row of likely causes — per-provider error rate and latency, DB pool in_use/max, consumer lag, restarts — with deploy annotations on every time series and links to runbooks and deeper dashboards. Node CPU and most of the 46 panels move off the main view, and they may note that the CPU page is cause-based. A competent generalist produces a long, evenly weighted panel list organised by component, uses average latency or raw 5xx thresholds, and doesn't connect the dashboard to the SLO or error budget.
