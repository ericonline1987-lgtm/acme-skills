## Grader basis

- decision-first: BLUF (bottom line and required action/decision with deadline first); HBR on stating purpose up front; Slideworks on executive summary giving the answer first.
- stale-estimate-flagged: analyst practice of surfacing the assumption a decision rests on; plain-language guidance to write for what the reader needs to decide (digital.gov audience principle). This is the case's main judgment point.
- length: the requester's stated constraint (~150 words, phone reading); digital.gov "summarize long documents at the beginning".
- noise-excluded: Slideworks "remove facts and figures that don't support" the message.

## Why a capable general model might miss it

Summarisers tend to compress every note faithfully and neutrally, so the conflict between "model built on Feb volumes" and "volume up 40%" is reported as two separate facts, or not at all, instead of as the reason the headline saving can't be trusted.

The notes and figures are synthetic.
