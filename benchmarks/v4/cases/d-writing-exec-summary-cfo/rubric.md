A senior operator's summary is built for a CFO reading on a phone: the first line is the decision and deadline plus the recommendation (take the one-year $1.02M bridge by Fri Nov 13 rather than lock in $1.18M/yr through 2029), followed by the two or three facts that justify it — chiefly that the BigQuery saving is unproven because the $760k model uses February volumes and load is up ~40%, plus migration cost, SQL rewrites and the unstarted InfoSec review — and what the bridge year buys. Irrelevant notes are dropped. A competent generalist typically writes a balanced chronological overview of both options, reports $760k (or a "$420k saving") as fact, and states the ask at the end.
