A senior reviewer blocks the PR: retrying a non-idempotent charge on timeout or 503 is exactly how customers get double-charged during a provider incident, so the request needs an idempotency key created once per charge attempt (e.g. from the order/payment-attempt ID) and reused on every retry. They also restrict retries to transient errors, replace fixed 500ms sleeps with capped exponential backoff and jitter, fit the total retry budget inside the 15s LB timeout (fewer attempts or shorter per-try timeout, or move charging to an async flow), drop the pointless sleep after the last attempt, and consider retry amplification if callers also retry. A competent generalist usually suggests backoff and maybe "only retry on 5xx", but treats the double-charge risk as secondary or proposes a key generated inside the retried function.
