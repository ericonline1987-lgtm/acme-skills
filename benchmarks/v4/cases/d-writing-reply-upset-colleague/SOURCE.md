## Grader basis

- owns-both-specifics: Lewicki study (OSU) — acknowledgment of responsibility is the most important element; CBS summary on phrasing it simply.
- fixes-date-himself: Lewicki study — offer of repair is the second most important element; the repair must fall on the person who made the commitment (practitioner judgment).
- credit-repair: offer of repair that matches the harm (public credit for a public omission) — OSU/CBS on repair.
- sounds-human: Readability Guidelines on vague phrases ("align", "liaise"); TechCrunch/Wikipedia on formulaic machine-sounding prose; CBS note that repeated apologies without change put pressure on the other person.

## Why a capable general model might miss it

Models default to validating, apology-heavy, therapeutic language and over-long explanations, and they often try to preserve the bad commitment by asking the other team to stretch.

The people and situation are synthetic.
