A senior manager's reply is a few direct lines in the sender's own voice: "You're right on both" — I should have named your team at all-hands and I shouldn't have given Kim a date without you; then the repair — I'll post credit in the channel today and tell Kim Dec 1 was my mistake — and a short ask to agree the real date together (Dec 15 or whatever the team can stand behind), with no pressure on the overworked designers. It owns the mistake without long justification or repeated apologies. A competent generalist typically writes a longer, padded message with validating phrases, a paragraph explaining the panic, an apology for her "feelings", or a request that the team "see what's possible" for Dec 1.
