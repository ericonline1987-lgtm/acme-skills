A senior incident communicator writes to the salon owner, not to engineers: what went wrong in one plain sentence (reminders went out at the wrong time, some twice), exactly when in the owner's own time zone, what was not affected (bookings, calendar, data), what the company is doing about money (automatic SMS credit on the December invoice), and — most importantly — the practical consequence the owner cares about: some of their clients may have missed appointments, we can't tell which, here's how to check and follow up. It apologises once, owns the mistake, and states realistic prevention steps without "never again". A competent generalist typically keeps UTC timestamps or engineering terms, leads with a technical explanation, and omits the missed-appointments consequence or hides it.
