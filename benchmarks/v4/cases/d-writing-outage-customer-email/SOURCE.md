## Grader basis

- local-time-window: plain-language "write for your audience" (digital.gov) applied to times; Atlassian templates ask for the time window of impact. The conversion uses US standard time (DST ended Nov 1, 2026).
- no-internal-jargon: 18F on jargon and unfamiliar technical terms; Atlassian "keep it simple and direct".
- missed-appointments-help: Atlassian on acknowledging and summarising the actual customer impact; writing for the reader's real consequence (digital.gov audience principle).
- scope-and-refund: basic correctness check against the notes (Atlassian: state impact and what was affected).
- no-overpromise: Atlassian postmortem guidance to keep the tone simple and avoid grand claims.

## Why a capable general model might miss it

Models summarising engineer notes tend to carry over UTC timestamps and technical cause, and focus on the vendor's failure rather than the downstream consequence for the customer's own clients.

The incident and notes are synthetic.
