## Grader basis

- no-hype: Mailchimp on not adding "hyperbolic language ... and over-promises"; TechCrunch/Wikipedia on commercial-sounding polish as a machine-writing tell.
- limits-included: writing for the actual audience (digital.gov) — managers deciding rollout need the constraints; Mailchimp on not over-promising.
- conflict-behaviour: 18F "be open and specific"; accuracy about product behaviour (practitioner judgment for release notes).
- concrete-opening: digital.gov main-point-first; TechCrunch/Wikipedia on generic statements of importance.
- no-invented-proof: Wikipedia field guide (via TechCrunch) on padded notability; basic integrity of attributed quotes.

## Why a capable general model might miss it

The brief's "make it pop" pulls models toward launch-post clichés and invented social proof, and they routinely drop or soften limitations that make a launch sound smaller.

The product, customer and facts are synthetic.
