## Grader basis

- answer-first: Slideworks (executive summary first so the reader gets the answer up front); BLUF.
- action-titles: Slideworks ("action title" articulating the so-what; storyline readable from titles alone).
- misses-up-front: BLUF / main point first applied to board reporting; Slideworks SCR storyline (complication stated early). Candour with boards is practitioner judgment.
- ask-follows-evidence: Slideworks (every element supports the title's insight; storyline flows to the resolution).
- fits-eight: basic correctness check against the stated constraint.

## Why a capable general model might miss it

Models reproduce the generic board-deck template (agenda → highlights → financials → asks) with topic titles and a positive framing, which hides the causal chain the asks depend on.

The company figures are synthetic.
