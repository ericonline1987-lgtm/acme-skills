## Grader basis

- facts-preserved: basic correctness check (the request says "keep all the actual info").
- deadline-up-front: plain-language "main point first" and front-loading the action (digital.gov principles).
- no-inflated-language: Wikipedia's AI-writing field guide as summarized by TechCrunch (inflated importance, "pivotal moment", commercial-sounding polish); Readability Guidelines and 18F on vague and figurative words; Mailchimp on avoiding hyperbole.
- nothing-added: editing practice — a rewrite must not introduce claims the author did not make (18F "be specific" applies to what is actually known).

## Why a capable general model might miss it

Models often treat "make it sound human" as a vocabulary swap, keeping the original structure (hype first, deadline mid-paragraph) and a softened rallying close, which still reads as machine-written.
